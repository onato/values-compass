Values Compass

A short self-assessment that maps what you value in public life. The result is a profile you can use to compare political parties and candidates against your own values, rather than against slogans.

  • 50 statements, about 10 minutes.
  • Answer on a five-point scale from strongly disagree to strongly agree. Go with your first reaction.
  • There are no right answers. The statements are about principles, not any particular country or party.
  • Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
