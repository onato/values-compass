Find the parties that fit your values, and see the evidence.

Most voting guides ask what you think about this year's policies. This one asks what you value when things collide: freedom or safety, growth or nature, tradition or change. Then it shows which New Zealand parties and candidates pull the same way, and lets you check every claim.

60 statements, about 12 minutes. Go with your first reaction. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

    How it works

    1. You answer 60 statements about principles, not parties or policies, on a five-point scale from strongly disagree to strongly agree. They build your profile on twelve dimensions, each a trade-off with two poles, defined in the methodology below.
    2. We compare your profile with the parties. Each party is scored on the same twelve dimensions from its manifesto, its record and its votes, with every source archived and listed. Where your electorate has been researched, candidates are scored from their own statements.
    3. You see who fits, and why. A ranked list with the dimensions you agree and disagree on, the reasoning behind every number, and a ready-made prompt so an AI of your choice can check the analysis independently.

    Why sixty statements?

    Five statements per dimension, at least two of them phrased from the opposite side, so no single careless answer can move a score by more than a fifth, and a habit of agreeing with everything lands you in the middle rather than at one end. That is what makes the profile worth comparing against parties that have been scored with the same care. Shorter tests are quicker and worse.

    Which parties fit your values

    Alignment is how close a party sits to your profile across the twelve dimensions, weighted by how strongly you lean. Where a party or candidate has no score on a dimension, that dimension counts as unknown and pulls the figure toward 50, so thin evidence cannot look like a strong match. Every score is our reading of the party's programme and record; open a party to see the evidence for each dimension, read , or browse all the sources. Candidates are not scored; the research prompt below covers them.

    Your electorate

    Under MMP the electorate vote chooses your local MP. Candidates are scored only where they have said something themselves; every other cell inherits the party's score with lower confidence, and candidates from unscored parties are scored only on their own statements.
