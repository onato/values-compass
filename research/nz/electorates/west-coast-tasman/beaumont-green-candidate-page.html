Andrew Beaumont

West Coast-Tasman

Candidate Bio
Donate
Volunteer
Events
Committed to supporting community and environment. An active listener, advocate, and good mediator/communicator.

I'm willing to stand up for what is good and just and right. I'm currently supporting and encouraging local communities to help protect our environment and our food security.

I will liaise with local government to develop and create jobs. The ultimate is that everyone can have a happy, healthy lifestyle in the communities they choose to live in.

I want to empower our young people to be leaders and participators in the democratic process. I am easy to talk to and confident in my ability to speak to all people with respect.
