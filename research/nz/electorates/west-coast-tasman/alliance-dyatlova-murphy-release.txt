 Alliance Party Announces Sara Dyatlova–Murphy As Candidate For West Coast – Tasman

 Thursday, 30 July 2026, 9:22 am

 Press Release: New Zealand Alliance Party

 The Alliance Party has announced software developer,
community advocate, and mother Sara Dyatlova–Murphy as its
candidate for the West Coast – Tasman electorate in the
2026 General Election.
Born and raised in Nelson with
personal and family roots in Golden Bay, Motueka, Riwaka,
and the West Coast, Ms Dyatlova–Murphy brings a rich blend
of practical experience and grassroots organising.
Her
background spans technology and software development
alongside hands-on work in hospitality and seasonal fruit
harvesting in local orchards.
Currently living in
Riwaka as a stay-at-home mother, she understands the
everyday pressures facing households across the
region.
Her commitment to public advocacy is shaped by
both local history and international perspective. Having
experienced the harsh economic impact of the National
Government’s 1991 “mother of all budgets” on
households during her childhood, she later spent time living
in Denmark, The Netherlands, the United Kingdom, and
Australia.
Her time in Denmark demonstrated firsthand
the high standard of security achieved through a well-funded
social democracy.
Ms Dyatlova–Murphy says the West
Coast – Tasman electorate is geographically large and
diverse, but whether people live in Golden Bay, Motueka, or
on ‘the Coast’, they all share common
struggles.
“People across our region need access to
timely healthcare, relief from cost-of-living pressures,
affordable housing, and secure, well-paid jobs. As a mother,
worker, and local resident, I am standing with the Alliance
because I believe our communities deserve strong
representation that fights for public ownership, workers’
rights, and a better future for our Tamariki."

Ms
Dyatlova–Murphy’s campaign will champion public
services, disability rights, regional healthcare access,
free education, public ownership of key assets, and economic
security for regional workers.
Alliance Party Leader
Victor Billot welcomed her candidacy.
“The Alliance
is very happy to announce Sara Dyatlova–Murphy as the
Alliance candidate for West Coast – Tasman,” he
says.
“Sara brings real-world experience, deep roots
across the region, and an unshakeable commitment to a better
New Zealand. At a time when working families are bearing the
brunt of economic pressure, Sara offers the practical,
compassionate leadership we need.”
The Alliance
Party extends its gratitude to previous candidate Louis
Coup, who has stepped down from the race due to
unavailability.
