Our Māori Manifesto

Our vision is an Aotearoa that honours Te Tiriti o Waitangi, where power and wealth sit with our communities, and where people and the rest of nature thrive together.

Aotearoa has enough for all of us. Our government should work for people and planet, not the greed of corporations and their shareholders.

For the past three years we’ve seen a Government on the side of the super-rich and the most powerful corporations.

Climate action cut, protections for nature gutted, and decisions handed to lobbyists and donors while families struggle with power bills, rent and groceries.

We’ve been told our entire lives that a fairer country is impossible by those profiting from things staying exactly as they are: unequal and unfair.

But New Zealanders know better. Politics belongs to those who turn up, and in communities across the motu, people are turning up demanding action.

This manifesto is our plan for a Green government to harness the energy for change, built on three priorities: affordable living, healthy nature and real democracy.

Affordable living, because we can have affordable electricity, housing, transport and groceries if we take on mega-corporate profits, and only the Green Party are willing to do that. 

Healthy nature, because since the beginning we have worked to protect and restore our water, forests, air and soil. And real democracy, because laws should be made for regular New Zealanders, not those who can afford to buy access to shape the rules in their favour.

Paid for by a plan that will tax the super-rich and mega-corporations, help fund critical social services, and cut income tax for 96 percent of people.

None of this would be possible without the hopes, ideas and passion of Green Party members, and the movement they’ve built. This manifesto is their work, and their commitment to change in Aotearoa.

That change is not hypothetical. Last time the Greens were in Government, we took more action on climate than the previous three decades of governments combined, secured the biggest ever boost to funding for protecting native plants and wildlife, and delivered Aotearoa’s first plan to eliminate family and sexual violence.

A party vote for the Green Party puts more Green MPs and Ministers around the decision-making table to reverse the damage from the last three years and fix the rules so they’re on side of people and planet.

Outlined in this document is a plan to do what is necessary. Protect what we all love. Ensure everyone’s needs are met. And make life better for all of us here in Aotearoa.

Marama Davidson | Chlöe Swarbrick
Green Party Co-Leaders
