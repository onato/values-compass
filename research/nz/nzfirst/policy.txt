 New Zealand First Policy

 Since 1993, New Zealand First has proven to consistently focus our efforts on five main policy pillars to advance and strengthen the social and economic direction of our country.

 Breaking up the Gentailers

 We will be breaking up the power companies so they can no longer control both the power and the price.

 Break up the Supermarket Duopoly

 For too long, New Zealanders have faced rising grocery bills while Woolworths and Foodstuffs control more than 80 percent of the grocery market. The Commerce Commission has previously found those supermarket giants earning around $1 million a day in excess profits.

 Returning 50% of mining royalties to the regions

 New Zealand First has announced its campaign policy that will see 50% of any royalties gained from mining to be directly returned to the region from which it came – not Wellington.
