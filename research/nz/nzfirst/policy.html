New Zealand First Policy

Since 1993, New Zealand First has proven to consistently focus our efforts on five main policy pillars to advance and strengthen the social and economic direction of our country.

Breaking up the Gentailers

We will be breaking up the power companies so they can no longer control both the power and the price.

Read More

Break up the Supermarket Duopoly

For too long, New Zealanders have faced rising grocery bills while Woolworths and Foodstuffs control more than 80 percent of the grocery market. The Commerce Commission has previously found those supermarket giants earning around $1 million a day in excess profits.

Read More

Returning 50% of mining royalties to the regions

New Zealand First has announced its campaign policy that will see 50% of any royalties gained from mining to be directly returned to the region from which it came – not Wellington.

Read More

Establishing a KiwiSaver Generation

New Zealand First is going to make KiwiSaver enrolment compulsory at birth and there will be an automatic immediate Crown contribution of $1000 for New Zealand citizens only.

Read More

Backing a New Zealand Owned Bank

New Zealand First will be proposing a buy-back of the Bank of New Zealand from National Australia Bank.

Read More

Disestablish Auckland’s ‘Independent Maori Statutory Board’

New Zealand First has announced a campaign policy to disestablish the unelected Auckland Council Independent Māori Statutory Board (IMSB).

Read More

Referendum on Maori Seats

The time has come for a decision on the future of the Māori seats.

Read More

Establishing a Special Economic Zone at Marsden Point

We are going to establish a Special Economic Zone at Marsden Point.

Read More

Complete the Christchurch Cathedral

New Zealand First Leader Winston Peters has announced a campaign commitment to fund an extra $15 million for the Christ Church Cathedral to get the rebuild moving.

Read More

Citizen’s Only Voting

New Zealand First has announced that we will be campaigning to change the electoral law to ensure that only citizens have the right to vote.

Read More

Future Proofing our Fuel Security

New Zealand First will be campaigning on an initial investment of $1 billion over the term of government, into a ‘National Subsurface Development Survey’ - to identify and create one national dataset across energy, geothermal and CO₂ storage sites, and create the framework for New Zealand to develop those sites, extract those resources, and build our country’s resilience and prosperous economic future.

Read More

The ‘Kiwi Kids Grant’

New Zealand First will campaign on introducing a ‘Kiwi Kids Grant’ that will support New Zealand citizens raising the first three children, through the first three years of their lives.

Read More

'Use It or Lose It' dairy policy

New Zealand First is proposing changes to the Dairy Industry Restructuring Act 2001 to incentivise investment in value-added dairy manufacturing in New Zealand, New Zealand First Leader Rt Hon Winston Peters has announced.

Read More

We Will Withdraw From The Paris Accord

New Zealand First is saying we need to get out of the Paris Accord altogether

Read More

The $100bn New Zealand Future Fund

We will establish a “New Zealand Future Fund” of up to $100 Billion to invest solely in a multi-decade infrastructure build, to ensure our future infrastructure security and to enable future economic growth and social enablement.
