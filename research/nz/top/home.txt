 I want to see long term, multi-party decisions made that consider both current and future generations.

 I believe the party's long term focus will make our country the best it can be.

 If we get some working NZers into the next Government, we might get the next Government working for NZers.

 I want Aotearoa to be guided by grounded, evidence-based policy that delivers the greatest amount of good for the greatest amount of people.

 I want my children (and grandchildren and great grandchildren) to live in a world that is cleaner, fairer, more equitable and more peaceful than what we have now.

 I support Opportunity because Opportunity has the most comprehensive policies to support nature.

 What a refreshing positive approach you guys are bringing to NZ politics! Thank you!!

 When I look for a party that cares about our stories (past, present and future), wants unity, and follows the evidence, Opportunity is it.

 I'd like to see politics in New Zealand become more forward-thinking.

 New Zealand needs vision, fresh and future focussed thinking to get us out of the downward spiral we are in.

 A fairer New Zealand has cleaned up its waters, politics and energy. Our circular economy is world-leading, our kids are thriving and our communities are stronger.

 This time I really believe we will get to Wellington and start to make a difference to NZ's future.

 Politics business as usual is broken. We need a complete democratic reset. No lobbyists, science and evidence led policies and with input from informed citizens.

 I don't just want a better New Zealand for my generation. I want to help build it.

 I want my daughter to grow up in a country that actually listens to her.
